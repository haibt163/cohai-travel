# CoHai Travel — P2 Migration Decision Matrix

Last reviewed: 11 September 2026.

## Status

This is the row-level **P2.15 working decision matrix** derived from the sanitized legacy source inventory produced from `haibt163/travel:data_vietaustravel`. It is deliberately conservative: `rewrite` means the subject is potentially reusable but legacy copy/facts must be reconstructed; `archive` means retain as source evidence without publishing; `discard` means do not migrate; `merge` consolidates a duplicate/locality into an identified canonical target.

**Important:** these dispositions are based on source-inventory evidence and current CoHai schema/seed evidence. They are not a claim that every target record is publication-ready. Media provenance, factual verification, exact URL behavior and final editorial approval remain separate gates.

## Decision rules

- No historical customer PII, credentials or raw booking records are migrated.
- Legacy placeholder/demo/test content is discarded rather than prettified into production content.
- Content with a valid subject but contaminated or untrusted legacy copy is `rewrite`.
- Duplicates or locality-level records are `merge` only when a canonical target is explicitly identified.
- Legacy lodging/room records are `archive` pending provenance, licensing and geographic verification.
- Reviews attributed to admin/test accounts are discarded.
- Canonical URLs must ultimately be locale-prefixed `/en/...` or `/vn/...` routes.
